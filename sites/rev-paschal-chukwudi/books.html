How it works

Two ways to receive.

01

Pick the title and the format

Every book shows two prices: one for the printed copy, one for the soft copy. Choose the one you want and the order form opens with the price already filled in.

02

Send the order and the payment

The form composes an email with your details and the total. Pay into the ministry account, then send the order — the reference is your name, so the office can match it.

03

Receive it

Soft copies arrive by email once payment is confirmed. Printed copies are dispatched after the office confirms the delivery fee for your address — you are told the cost before anything is sent.

Nothing is charged automatically on this site. No card details are collected here — payment goes to the ministry account shown in the order form, the same account used for giving.
